# KOORO Studio — Brand Platform

## Name

**KOORO Studio**

## Descriptor

**Web Services**

## Claim

**From locals to locals.**

## Positioning

KOORO Studio builds editorial, useful websites for independent local businesses: shops, studios, restaurants, clinics, schools, makers and service companies that need to be found, trusted and contacted without looking like a generic template.

The brand lives between two ideas:

1. **Local understanding** — knowing how people actually search, compare, book, message and visit a neighbourhood business.
2. **Elevated execution** — typography, rhythm, copy, imagery and interaction with the restraint of a design studio, not a cheap landing factory.

## Promise

Local businesses deserve serious internet.

KOORO turns a business into a clear digital front door: what it is, why it matters, how to contact it, and what action the visitor should take next.

## Voice

- Editorial, direct, compact.
- Premium but not corporate.
- Local but not small.
- Technical confidence without technical language.
- No “growth hacking”, no startup filler, no template vocabulary.

## Core copy system

- **Hero:** Web services from locals to locals.
- **Support:** We design and build sharp, practical websites for independent businesses that need to be found, trusted and contacted.
- **Services:** Identity, copy, web design, local SEO, forms, booking flows, maps, analytics and light automation.
- **Portfolio placeholder language:** Concepts, studies, systems and future case slots — not current client work.

## Visual direction

- Minimal editorial/brutalist typography.
- Heavy wordmark, tight tracking, black on warm off-white.
- Small mono metadata, big confident blocks, generous negative space.
- Placeholder imagery can be abstract/editorial; no low-end landing screenshots on the front page.

## Logo direction

Typographic wordmark only: **KOORO** as a compact black editorial block. The two O forms can act as modular counters/windows: local doors, routes, portals. No icon mascot, no gradient symbol, no SaaS badge.

## Frontpage rule

Do not show the current raw local demos as portfolio work. They are useful for production learning, but KOORO’s public surface should feel more elevated. Use placeholder studies until stronger work exists.
